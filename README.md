☕ Daily Brew Coach — All-In-One Personal Life Coach & Routine Builder

A high-energy, pocket-sized life coach and routine engine that combines time-adaptive dashboards, 2-minute micro-coaching, focus tools with ambient audio synthesizers, scientific workout guides, study trackers, and gamified habit building into a tactile, neo-brutalist interface.

🌟 Product Overview

Daily Brew Coach is an all-in-one daily life coach designed to build consistency and positive routines without overwhelming cognitive load. Inspired by the tactile, punchy aesthetic of platforms like Buy Me a Coffee and Duolingo, it turns daily self-improvement into bite-sized, gamified wins.

Whether packaged as a native Android APK (via WebView/Capacitor) or served as a progressive web app (PWA), Daily Brew Coach operates offline-first using local browser storage and browser-native Web Audio & Speech APIs.

✨ Key Features

☀️ 1. Time-Adaptive Command Center

Dynamic Time Modes: Automatically shifts theme styling, greeting copy, and primary action cards between Morning Mode (intent & micro-coaching), Daytime Mode (high-energy Pomodoro sprints), and Evening Mode (reflection & wind-down).

Manual Preview Switcher: Quick toggle icon to preview any time mode on demand.

🎧 2. 2-Minute Micro-Coach

Audio & Speech Engine: Built-in Speech Synthesis with variable speed controls (1.0x – 2.0x).

Live Transcript Syncing: Scrollable transcript reader synced to the audio session.

Coaching Library: Pre-loaded lessons covering friction reduction, deep work shields, evening shutdown rituals, and morning momentum.

⚡ 3. Focus Sprint & Ambient Audio Synthesizer

Tactile Pomodoro Timer: Configurable modes for Focus (25m), Short Break (5m), and Long Break (15m).

Synthesized Ambient Audio: Zero external audio files required! Generates real-time soundscapes using the Web Audio API:

🌧️ Rain Noise

🌊 Ocean Waves

☕ Coffee Shop Drone

🧠 10Hz Alpha Binaural Beats

Volume Slider: Independent ambient audio gain control.

🏋️‍♂️ 4. Scientific Fitness & Workout Suite

Targeted Warmups (5m & 10m): Warmup routines specifically linked to workout splits (Chest Warmup, Legs Warmup, Upper Body Warmup, Full Body Warmup).

Comprehensive Exercise Splits: Workouts categorized by Chest & Triceps, Back & Biceps, Legs & Core, and Shoulders.

Traditional Yoga Flows: Includes Surya Namaskar (Sun Salutation 12-Pose Flow), Shirshasana (Headstand Prep), and Bedtime Sleep Unwind.

Targeted Muscle Stretching: Specific stretches for chest/shoulders, hamstrings, and lower back decompression.

Tiered Calisthenics Progressions: Skill-classified routines across Beginner, Intermediate, and Advanced levels.

Scientific Exercise Breakdown Modals: Click any exercise to inspect:

🧬 Biomechanical & Scientific Explanation

⚠️ Form Mistakes & What to Avoid

🎯 Standard Targets (Reps, Sets, & Tempo)

🎓 5. Mind & Study Hub

Reading & Deep Learning Tracker: Track books, chapters, and target page goals with completion checkboxes.

Course Progress Tracker: Visual module completion progress bars with increment/decrement controls.

💡 6. Ideas, Goals & Ambition

Brainstorming Canvas: Capture raw project ideas, break down step-by-step action plans, and track progress status (Planned, In Progress, Completed).

💼 7. Job Application Tracker

Career Pipeline: Track job applications across stages (Applied, Interview Scheduled, Not Responded).

Status Filter Pills: Instantly filter your job search pipeline.

✍️ 8. Gratitude & Mood Canvas

Mood Logger: 5 emoji mood states (😍, 🙂, 😐, 😔, 😤).

Reflection Prompts: Configurable reflection prompts with persistent history logging.

👁️ 9. Declutterable Power Hubs & Dynamic Dock Navigation

Eye Icon Toggle (👁️): Hide or reveal any hub card on the dashboard to declutter your workspace.

Scrollable Dock / Sidebar: Toggling a hub's visibility dynamically adds/removes its shortcut icon from the mobile horizontally scrollable footer dock and the desktop sidebar.

🛠️ Tech Stack & Architecture

Frontend: Single-File HTML5 + JavaScript (ES6+)

Styling: Tailwind CSS (via CDN) with Neo-Brutalist design tokens (4px borders, #FFDD00 primary yellow, stark drop shadows)

Typography: Fredoka (chunky, rounded headings) & Nunito (soft, legible body text) via Google Fonts

Icons & Visuals: Lucide Icons CDN & Canvas Confetti

Audio Synthesis: Web Audio API (white noise, binaural beats, biquad filtering) & Web Speech API (Text-to-Speech)

Storage: Browser localStorage (Offline-first data persistence).
